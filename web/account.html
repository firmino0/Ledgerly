Ledgerly

Your account

Your own private Ledgerly, using your own wallet. You sign every transaction yourself.

Accounts are not switched on for this site.

Before you start

  • This is experimental software. Transactions use real funds from your own wallet on Robinhood Chain.
  • You sign every transaction yourself. Ledgerly never holds your keys and cannot move your money.
  • There is no email, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered. Your wallet and funds are not affected.
  • Start with a small amount. Nothing here is financial advice.
